My Story

Why I Started The Agile Soul

For more than 7 years I have worked with women of every age and fitness level. Again and again I saw the same thing: generic workouts and crash diets don't last, because they aren't built for the person doing them.

The Agile Soul is my answer to that. Every session is one-on-one and online, so you get my full attention, a plan made only for you, and the freedom to train from home, at a time that suits you.

A stronger body, a calmer mind and food that works for you, not against you.
