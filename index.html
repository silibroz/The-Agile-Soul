FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior experience or a particular fitness level to get started?

Not at all! The sessions are suitable for beginners as well as individuals with prior fitness experience. Your routine will be planned according to your current fitness level, lifestyle and personal goals.

I have a busy work schedule. Are the class timings flexible?

Absolutely! We understand that balancing work and personal commitments can be challenging. Flexible scheduling options are available to help you maintain a consistent wellness routine without disrupting your daily schedule.

What if I miss a scheduled class due to work or an emergency?

We understand that unexpected meetings and personal commitments can come up. If you miss a session, please inform us in advance whenever possible. Make-up or rescheduling options can be discussed based on availability and the class plan.

Do you provide personalised diet plans?

Personalised diet planning is included as part of our Yoga + Functional Training Combo programme. This helps align your nutrition with your fitness goals and training routine. Diet plans are not included with standalone yoga sessions.

Can I join if my main goal is weight loss or improving fitness?

Yes! Whether your goal is weight management, improved flexibility, better stamina, stress reduction or overall fitness, your routine can be tailored to support your individual needs.

How much time do I need to dedicate every day?

You don't need to spend hours exercising. The routine is designed around your availability and goals. Even a manageable, consistent practice can help you build healthier habits over time.

Are the sessions conducted online, and what do I need to participate?

Yes, sessions are conducted online, so you can participate from home or wherever you feel comfortable. You will need a stable internet connection, a smartphone or laptop, comfortable clothing and a little space to practise.

How do I get started, and can I discuss my goals before joining?

Of course! You can get in touch to discuss your lifestyle, work schedule, fitness goals and expectations. Based on the discussion, we can help you choose a suitable programme.
